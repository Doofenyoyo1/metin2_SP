# MT2009_PLUS_BOSS_CHESTS_V1: the boss chests of the Razador (6091) and Nemere (6191)
# dungeons (the operator, 29 September): Skrzynia Razadora (50270) and Skrzynia Nemere
# (50271), the official vnums. razador_dungeon.quest / nemere_dungeon.quest give one to
# every player of the party inside the instance when the boss dies. Each chest is a Pct
# group: its lines roll one after another (percent, 1-100), each one opening a nested
# group ("s<vnum>") that gives ONE item by weight:
#   * the gear (always): Razador - the level-65 weapons and the level-61 armours,
#     Nemere - the level-70 weapons and the Titan shield, +0..+4;
#   * the filler (always, and a second one at 40% / 50%): the cheap part of the
#     official chests (pl-wiki Skrzynia Razadora / Skrzynia Nemere), without the
#     level-75 weapons, the level-66 armours ("stalki") and the Skrzynia Mroku;
#   * a pet item (50% / 60%): the New Pet System's books, elixirs, snacks, the book
#     chest and, rarely, the boss's own egg.
# The nested groups' numbers (95027x) are no item: the reader keeps the first group
# per number, and a number under 30000 would register the items as a unique group.
